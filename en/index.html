01About

Our vision is to deliver technologically advanced, science-based research services to the private and public sectors in urban planning, agriculture, disaster risk, environment and education. With new technology we lighten the load in these fields — the problems that look overwhelming become ours to solve.

Founded in 2021.

01 / 03 — GIS analysis & remote sensing

Spatial patterns that tables cannot show: suitability, siting, routing, cadastre, NDVI and snow-cover monitoring.

  • GIS analysis
  • Remote sensing (RS)
More

02 / 03 — Water resources & flood modelling

Catchment and river-flow studies to international standards, plus 1D/2D flood hazard modelling on high-resolution terrain.

  • Hydrological studies
  • Flood modelling
More

03 / 03 — GIS education

Courses from beginner to practitioner level, built on real project data — mapping, spatial analysis, hydraulic modelling.

  • GIS courses
