01/03

QGIS · ArcGIS Pro · Earth Engine

Geographic information system (GIS) services

Spatial patterns that tables cannot show: suitability, siting, routing, cadastre, NDVI and snow-cover monitoring.

Our GIS analysis services turn data into a strategic tool, letting you see the spatial relationships and patterns that stay invisible in tables and text.

  • Agriculture: Crop suitability analysis.
  • Urban planning: Siting of infrastructure with urban-planning constraints applied.
  • Logistics: Optimising delivery routes and cutting costs.
  • Public sector: Urban planning and cadastral data management.
  • Environment: Ecosystem protection and pollution mapping.

Remote sensing solutions make it possible to monitor large areas dynamically, without a physical presence on site and without the logistics costs.

  • NDVI index: Remote assessment of plant health and growth rates.
  • Water sector: Analysis of snow-cover dynamics in selected areas.
  • Forest fund: Preliminary assessment of fire hotspots.
  • Soil moisture: Soil-moisture assessment for irrigated areas.
  • Disaster monitoring: Mapping the consequences of floods and landslides.
02/03

HEC-RAS · HEC-HMS · DEM / DTM

Water resources and hydrological services

Catchment and river-flow studies to international standards, plus 1D/2D flood hazard modelling on high-resolution terrain.

We carry out high-accuracy hydrological modelling of catchments and river flows, using software packages that meet international standards.

  • Energy: Flow assessment and optimisation for hydropower plants.
  • Water supply: Allocation of drinking- and irrigation-water resources.
  • Engineering design: Complete hydrological studies for hydro-engineering projects.
  • Agriculture: Planning irrigation systems according to the terrain.
  • Research: The impact of climate change on the water sector.

We carry out 1D/2D hydraulic modelling of flood risk, based on high-resolution topographic data (DEM/DTM) and rainfall-intensity scenarios.

  • Hazard assessment: Assessing the probability and severity of floods.
  • Planning: Creating strategic flood-protection maps.
  • Urban environment: Modelling how floods form in the urban environment.
  • Insurance: Assessing infrastructure made vulnerable by water-related disasters.
03/03

Hands-on · Web maps

GIS education

Courses from beginner to practitioner level, built on real project data — mapping, spatial analysis, hydraulic modelling.

We run specialised courses in QGIS, ArcGIS Pro, HEC-RAS and Google Earth Engine, from beginners to experienced professionals.

  • Hands-on learning: Working with real projects and databases.
  • Mastering the tools: Studying leading GIS and hydrological software packages.
  • Cartography: Producing final maps, both web and static.
  • Spatial analysis: Applying data-processing and statistical methods.
