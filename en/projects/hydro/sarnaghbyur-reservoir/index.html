Hydrological studies · 2025

Hydrological study of the Sarnaghbyur reservoir catchment

Client
Modul LLC
Year
2025
Duration
3 months

As part of the project, the hydrological elements of the reservoir's catchment were analysed. The dependability of the mean flow and of maximum discharges was calculated to both the SNiP and the ICOLD standard. Rainfall–runoff modelling was carried out. The minimum discharge and the possible climate impacts were assessed by scenario.

Tools & methods

  • HEC-HMS
  • QGIS
  • RMC-BestFit
  • GEE
