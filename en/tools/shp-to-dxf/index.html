How to use it

01 / 03

Put the parcel and building layers (.shp, .shx, .dbf, .prj) in one zip with a Latin name.

02 / 03

Upload the archive, up to 7 MB, choose the entity type and pay.

03 / 03

Get five DXF files: lines and vertices per layer, plus the combined _cad_code.

Entity typeClosed polyline (POLYLINE)Separate lines (LINE)
OptionsKeep Z elevationsCoordinates are not reprojected: the DXF keeps the original system.

Privacy

Files are not sent to a server. The conversion happens entirely in your browser.

Limits

Maximum size 7 MB. Use a Latin-letter file name.

Limitation of liability

The conversion runs in your browser, on the files you upload. GeoGeeks LLC does not receive, store or control them, and accepts no responsibility for the accuracy of the result or for how it is used. Coordinates are not reprojected.

Inner holes become closed lines too, and each vertex is written once. Cadastre codes appear only in _cad_code, on their own layers.
